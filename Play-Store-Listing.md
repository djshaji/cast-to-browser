**Cast to Browser** turns your Android phone into a powerful local media server. Stream videos and photos from your device to any web browser on the same Wi-Fi network — no Chromecast, Apple TV, or streaming hardware required.

**How It Works:**
1. Open the app and grant media permissions
2. Copy the URL displayed in the app (e.g., http://192.168.1.5:8080)
3. Paste the URL into any browser on your Wi-Fi network
4. Tap a video or photo in the app's gallery to start casting
5. Use the on-screen remote to play, pause, or seek


**Key Features:**
• **Local Media Streaming** — Stream directly from your device's gallery. No uploading, no cloud, no waiting.
• **Real-time Remote Control** — Play, pause, seek, and monitor progress instantly from the app.
• **Secure & Private** — All streaming happens on your local Wi-Fi network only. Your media never touches the internet.
• **Works on Any Browser** — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge—use any modern web browser on any device.
• **Fast Seeking** — Built on HTTP Partial Content streaming for instant seeking even in long videos.
• **Material Design 3** — Beautiful, modern interface that adapts to phones and tablets.
• **Share Support** — Share media from Google Photos, Gallery, or other apps directly into Cast to Browser.
• **Optional Pro Upgrade** — Unlock additional features with a one-time in-app purchase.

**Tech Highlights:**
• Built with Jetpack Compose for a smooth, responsive UI
• Embedded Ktor HTTP/WebSocket server
• Real-time telemetry and playback synchronization
• Dynamic port selection (works even if port 8080 is busy)

**Requirements:**
• Android 10 or higher
• Wi-Fi network (local only; no internet needed for casting)
• Any modern web browser on your second device

**Privacy:**
Cast to Browser does not collect any personal data. It uses no analytics, no ads, and no trackers. Your media is streamed only within your local network and never uploaded or shared. See the full privacy policy on GitHub.
